# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 3, 2026**

## Overview

Harjun iltasoitto ("the app") is a simple alarm application that plays the Harjun iltasoitto bell sound at a scheduled time each day.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. No analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind are included.

All preferences (whether the alarm is active, the alarm time, and the volume setting) are stored locally on your device only and never leave it.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions, all of which are used solely for its core functionality:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Re-schedule the alarm after the device restarts |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Ensure the device wakes up to play the alarm |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | Schedule the alarm to fire at the exact configured time |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Display a notification while the alarm is playing so you can stop it |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` | Play audio reliably in the background |

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs.

Google Play may collect certain information in connection with the distribution of the app (installs, crashes, ratings). This is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
